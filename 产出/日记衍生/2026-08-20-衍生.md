# 2026-08-20 日记衍生

## 【英文材料】

### 1. 口语短文（讲给朋友听的版本）

Yesterday I watched a live stream from a guy I really look up to, and it gave me a lot to think about. He was talking about this book I've been reading, *Designing Your Life*, and something clicked — I want to turn chapter four, the one about getting unstuck, into a tool that helps people find their direction when they feel lost. That chapter really spoke to me, because I know what it's like to feel stuck.

Then near the end, he shared some news that hit everyone: in about six months, we'll all have to leave the website we built together. Honestly, I felt a little sad hearing that. This place means something to us.

But then I thought about it, and you know what, it's not that big a deal. Even the Monkey King only found out how powerful he was after he left his master's mountain. No matter how hard it is to say goodbye, when the time comes, we all have to walk out that door. I just hope we meet again at the top, each of us living a life we're proud of.
