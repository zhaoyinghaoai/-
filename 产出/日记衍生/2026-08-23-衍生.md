# 2026-08-23 日记衍生

## 【英文材料】

### 1. 口语短文（约 230 词）

Something happened at work today, and the lesson cost 300 yuan — well, almost.

I run a small package pickup station. When a parcel sits around too long, I call the customer to come get it. Turns out, how you say it matters. A lot.

I used to just tell people straight up: "Your package's here, come pick it up." Apparently that rubs some people the wrong way. One complaint and I'm out 300 yuan, plus an earful from the boss. Not worth it. Not even close.

So here's the new script: instead of telling, I ask. "Would today work for you?" Same call, same package — but now it's their call, their schedule, and nobody feels pushed around.

Funny thing is, the cheapest fix was just changing how I ask. No new tool, no new system. Six words.

I did the math, and it got me thinking about every other routine line I say all day. Which ones are quietly saving me trouble, and which ones are quietly asking for it?

If a complaint ever does come, 300 yuan for one lesson feels expensive. But if it sticks — if I never make that call the old way again — that's the cheapest tuition I'll ever pay.

## 【一条朋友圈】

今天学了一课：打电话叫人来拿快递，别再用命令的口气。同一件事，「你的件到了，来取一下」和「您今天方便过来拿吗」，差得不是一点半点——一个招投诉，一个招人待见。说话这门功夫，处处值钱。

**短版（18 字）：**

一句话换个问法，省下的可能是 300 块。
